## Deskripsi
```
The Panama Canal just had its best year ever 🚢 When war choked the Strait of Hormuz, Asia started buying American oil and gas, and those tankers squeezed through Panama. Revenue hit a record 6.6 billion dollars in fiscal 2026 💰

But this canal doesn't run on fuel. It runs on rain 🌧️ Every ship that crosses can use around 50 million gallons of fresh water from Gatun Lake, the same lake that gives drinking water to about half of Panama 💧

Now El Niño is back 🔥 The canal has already cut how many ships can pass each day, and the dry season could start a month early. If the rain doesn't come, Panama has to choose: water for ships, or water for people. And by law, people come first ⚖️
```
